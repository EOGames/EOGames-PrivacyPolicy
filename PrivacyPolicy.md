# Privacy Policy — EkonkarGames

**Effective Date:** September 7, 2022  
**Last Updated:** November 2026  

**EkonkarGames** ("we", "our", or "us") develops and publishes games as ad-supported applications. These services are provided by EkonkarGames at no cost and are intended for use as is.

This page informs visitors and players regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of technical and device-level information for anyone using our mobile games and related services.

By installing, accessing, or playing our games, you consent to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. We do not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

Our games are designed for entertainment and do not require personal identification details such as your real name, physical address, email address, or phone number to play. 

However, our games integrate third-party software development kits (SDKs) and advertising mediation networks to deliver ads, reward players, optimize game performance, and analyze stability. These third-party services automatically collect certain non-personally identifiable information from your device, including:

* **Device Identifiers:** Android Advertising ID (AAID / GAID), unique device tokens, and hardware identifiers.
* **Device & Hardware Information:** Device manufacturer, model, operating system version, system language, carrier, and screen resolution.
* **Network & Approximate Location:** IP address, connection type (Wi-Fi, cellular), and approximate geographic location (country and city level).
* **Ad Interaction & Telemetry:** Impressions shown, click data, video view completions, and interaction with mediated advertisements.
* **Crash & Performance Data:** Application state during an unexpected crash, stack traces, frame rates, and ANR (Application Not Responding) diagnostics.

### 3. Purpose of Data Processing

The non-personal technical data collected by our third-party SDK partners is processed solely for legitimate operational purposes:
* **Ad Delivery & Monetization:** Displaying relevant banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads.
* **Frequency Capping & Fraud Prevention:** Preventing bot abuse, invalid clicks, and repeated ad spam.
* **Game Diagnostics:** Investigating software bugs, memory bottlenecks, and device-specific crashes.
* **Feature Optimization:** Understanding overall level difficulty and general gameplay engagement patterns.

### 4. Cookies and Local Storage

Our games do not use standard web browser cookies. However, local storage on your device and third-party SDK caching are used to remember your in-game progression, audio preferences, and high scores offline, as well as to cache ad creative assets for smooth playback.

---

### 5. Managing Your Privacy & Advertising ID

You have full control over the advertising identifier transmitted by your device. You can reset your Android Advertising ID or opt out of personalized interest-based advertising at any time directly through your device settings:
* Open your Android device **Settings**.
* Go to **Google > Ads** (or **Privacy > Ads** depending on your Android OS version).
* Tap **Delete advertising ID** or **Reset advertising ID**, or toggle **Opt out of Ads Personalization**.

When opted out, you will still see advertisements in the game, but they will be contextual rather than personalized to your interests.
